📚 Part 1｜單字學習
persevere
[ˌpɜrsɪˈvɪr]
v.
堅持不懈；鍥而不捨
🔴
衍生詞perseverance (n.) 毅力；堅持不懈｜persevering (adj.) 有毅力的
片語persevere in/with 在……方面堅持｜persevere despite difficulties 不畏困難地堅持
容易混淆persevere 強調面對困難仍繼續努力；persist 語氣較中性，也可指固執堅持錯的事
  • Despite numerous failures, she persevered and eventually became a celebrated scientist.
    儘管屢次失敗，她仍堅持不懈，最終成為一位備受讚譽的科學家。
  • Learning a new language is difficult, but those who persevere will see great results.
    學習新語言很困難，但那些堅持下去的人將會看到豐碩的成果。
  • His perseverance in the face of hardship inspired everyone around him.
    他在逆境中展現的毅力激勵了身邊的每一個人。
sacrifice
[ˈsækrɪˌfaɪs]
n. / v.
犧牲；奉獻（n./v. 同形）
🔴
衍生詞sacrificial (adj.) 作為犧牲的；奉獻的
片語make a sacrifice 做出犧牲｜sacrifice A for B 為了B犧牲A｜at the sacrifice of 以……為代價
  • She sacrificed her own safety to rescue children who were in danger.
    她犧牲了自身的安全，去拯救那些身陷危險的孩子。
  • The parents made many sacrifices so that their children could receive a good education.
    父母做出了許多犧牲，讓孩子們能夠接受良好的教育。
  • True leadership sometimes requires the willingness to sacrifice personal gain for the greater good.
    真正的領導有時需要為了更大的利益而願意犧牲個人利益。
bravery
[ˈbreɪvərɪ]
n.
勇敢；英勇
🔴
衍生詞brave (adj.) 勇敢的｜bravely (adv.) 勇敢地｜brave (v.) 勇於面對
容易混淆bravery 指面對恐懼時的勇氣；courage 較廣義，包含道德勇氣；boldness 有時偏「大膽莽撞」
片語an act of bravery 英勇行為｜bravery in the face of danger 面對危險的勇氣
  • Her bravery during the crisis saved the lives of many innocent people.
    她在危機中展現的英勇救了許多無辜人們的生命。
  • The firefighter was awarded a medal for his outstanding bravery.
    那位消防員因其傑出的英勇表現而獲頒獎章。
  • It takes real bravery to stand up for what is right when others stay silent.
    當其他人保持沉默時，為了正義挺身而出需要真正的勇氣。
courage
[ˈkɜrɪdʒ]
n.
勇氣；膽量
🟠
衍生詞courageous (adj.) 勇敢的｜courageously (adv.) 勇敢地｜encourage (v.) 鼓勵（en- + courage）
片語have the courage to V 有勇氣做……｜pluck up courage 鼓起勇氣｜moral courage 道德勇氣
  • It took great courage for her to speak out against injustice in public.
    她需要很大的勇氣才能在公開場合為不公義的事情發聲。
  • He finally plucked up the courage to ask for help after months of struggling alone.
    在獨自掙扎了幾個月之後，他終於鼓起勇氣開口求助。
  • The young soldier showed remarkable courage under extremely dangerous conditions.
    那位年輕士兵在極度危險的情況下展現出驚人的勇氣。
rescue
[ˈrɛskju]
n. / v.
救援；拯救（n./v. 同形）
🟠
衍生詞rescuer (n.) 救援者｜rescue operation 救援行動
片語come to the rescue 前來救援｜rescue sb from 從……中救出某人｜rescue mission 救援任務
  • Irena Sendler risked her life to rescue thousands of children during World War II.
    伊蓮娜‧森德勒冒著生命危險，在二次世界大戰期間拯救了數千名孩童。
  • The coast guard launched a rescue operation after receiving the distress signal.
    海岸防衛隊接到求救信號後，立即展開救援行動。
  • Nobody expected him to come to the rescue at such a critical moment.
    沒有人預料到他會在如此關鍵的時刻挺身救援。
inspire
[ɪnˈspaɪr]
v.
激勵；啟發；鼓舞
🟠
衍生詞inspiration (n.) 靈感；鼓舞｜inspiring (adj.) 激勵人心的｜inspired (adj.) 受啟發的
片語inspire sb to V 激勵某人做……｜draw inspiration from 從……汲取靈感
  • Her story of perseverance continues to inspire young people around the world.
    她堅持不懈的故事持續激勵著全世界的年輕人。
  • The teacher's passion for history inspired many students to study the subject seriously.
    那位老師對歷史的熱情激勵了許多學生認真鑽研這門學科。
  • What was your inspiration for writing this song?
    你寫這首歌的靈感是什麼？
admire
[ədˈmaɪr]
v.
欽佩；讚賞；仰慕
🟠
衍生詞admiration (n.) 欽佩；讚賞｜admirable (adj.) 令人欽佩的｜admirer (n.) 崇拜者
片語admire sb for 因……而欽佩某人｜hold sb in admiration 對某人懷抱欽佩
  • People around the world admire her for her extraordinary bravery and selfless sacrifice.
    世界各地的人們欽佩她非凡的勇敢精神和無私的奉獻。
  • I admire his ability to stay calm under pressure.
    我很欽佩他在壓力下保持冷靜的能力。
  • She has earned the admiration of her colleagues through years of hard work.
    她通過多年的努力工作贏得了同事們的欽佩。
dedicate
[ˈdɛdɪˌket]
v.
奉獻；致力於；獻給
🟠
衍生詞dedication (n.) 奉獻；敬業精神｜dedicated (adj.) 專心致志的
片語dedicate oneself to 致力於……｜be dedicated to 全心投入於……
  • She dedicated her entire life to helping children in need.
    她將一生奉獻給幫助有需要的孩子們。
  • The doctor is dedicated to finding a cure for the rare disease.
    那位醫生全心投入於尋找這種罕見疾病的治療方法。
  • His dedication to teaching has earned him the respect of three generations of students.
    他對教學的奉獻精神贏得了三代學生的尊重。
struggle
[ˈstrʌgəl]
n. / v.
掙扎；奮鬥；費力（n./v. 同形）
🟠
片語struggle to V 費力做……｜struggle against 奮力抵抗……｜struggle with 與……搏鬥
容易混淆struggle 強調努力而費力的過程；fight 強調主動積極的對抗；strive 較文雅，強調持續努力
  • Many survivors struggled to rebuild their lives after the disaster.
    許多倖存者在災難後艱難地重建自己的生活。
  • The young athlete struggled with injuries throughout the season but never gave up.
    那位年輕運動員整個賽季都在與傷病搏鬥，但始終沒有放棄。
  • The long struggle for equal rights changed the course of history.
    長期爭取平等權利的奮鬥改變了歷史的進程。
overcome
[ˌovərˈkʌm]
v.
克服；戰勝；壓倒
🟠
片語overcome obstacles/difficulties 克服障礙/困難｜overcome fear 克服恐懼｜be overcome by 被……所壓倒（情感）
容易混淆overcome 強調以努力超越困難；conquer 語氣較強，有「征服」意味；defeat 多指在競爭中擊敗對手
  • She overcame enormous obstacles to become the first woman in her field.
    她克服了巨大的障礙，成為她所在領域的第一位女性。
  • With bravery and perseverance, anyone can overcome even the toughest challenges.
    憑藉勇氣和毅力，任何人都能克服哪怕是最艱難的挑戰。
  • He was overcome with emotion when he finally reunited with his family.
    當他終於與家人團聚時，他情緒激動，難以自持。

✏️ Part 2｜文法加強 3 大重點
文法重點 1 ｜ 學測綜合測驗 — so...that / such...that 結果子句
so + adj/adv + that... ／ such + (a/an) + adj + N + that...

這兩個句型都用來表達「如此……以致於……」的結果，考試必考！關鍵是判斷 so 後面接的是形容詞/副詞，而 such 後面接的是名詞（片語）。

so + adj/adv + that + S + V
such + (a/an) + adj + N + that + S + V
such + adj + 不可數名詞/複數名詞 + that + S + V
She was so courageous that she never gave up, even when facing death.
她如此勇敢，即使面對死亡也從未放棄。（so + adj）
It was such a brave act that the whole world was moved by her story.
那是如此勇敢的行動，全世界都被她的故事感動了。（such + a + adj + N）
She made such great sacrifices that her name will never be forgotten.
她做出了如此巨大的犧牲，她的名字將永遠不會被遺忘。（such + adj + 複數名詞）

⚠️ 常見錯誤：把 such 和 so 對調。遇到空格後面是名詞，一定選 such；只有形容詞或副詞才選 so。

文法重點 2 ｜ 段考 Q25 — despite / in spite of ＋ 名詞 vs. although ＋ 子句
despite / in spite of 只能接名詞；although 才能接子句

這是段考讓步語氣題最常錯的一對！記住：despite / in spite of 後面只能接名詞片語或 V-ing；although / even though / though 後面才接完整子句（S + V）。

Despite / In spite of + 名詞（片語）/ V-ing, S + V...
Although / Even though / Though + S + V, S + V...
Despite the danger, she continued to rescue children from the occupied city.
儘管危機四伏，她仍繼續從被占領的城市中拯救孩子們。（despite + 名詞）
Although she knew the risk, she chose to persevere in her mission.
雖然她知道風險，她還是選擇堅持完成她的使命。（although + S + V）
In spite of struggling for years, she never stopped believing in what she was doing.
儘管奮鬥了多年，她從未停止相信自己所做的事情。（in spite of + V-ing）

⚠️ 常見錯誤：寫 "despite she was afraid"（❌），應改為 "despite her fear"（✅） 或 "although she was afraid"（✅）。

文法重點 3 ｜ 學測閱讀 / 段考 — It is + adj + for sb + to V
虛主詞 It 讓句子更流暢，不定詞才是真正主詞

當不定詞作主詞時，英文習慣用虛主詞 it 放句首，將不定詞後移，句子讀起來更自然。這是學測綜合測驗愛考的結構！

It is + adj + for sb + to V ...
（= To V ... is adj for sb.）
It is extremely difficult for ordinary people to overcome such extreme fear.
對普通人來說，要克服如此極端的恐懼是非常困難的。
It was impossible for anyone to imagine the bravery she showed during that dark period.
任何人都無法想像她在那段黑暗時期所展現出的英勇。
It takes real courage for a person to sacrifice their own comfort for the sake of others.
為了他人而犧牲自身的安逸，對一個人來說需要真正的勇氣。（it takes + N + for sb to V）

📌 進階用法：It takes + N + for sb + to V（需要……才能……）是此句型的重要變體，如：It takes courage for sb to speak up.

📖 Part 3｜閱讀測驗
主題：People & Courage（連結段考 Irena Sendler）約 113 字難度：高中

The Quiet Rescuer

During one of history's darkest periods, a young social worker made a decision that would change thousands of lives. Despite the enormous danger, she chose to rescue children who faced certain death. Each night, she struggled to smuggle children out of the occupied city, hiding them in bags, boxes, and even coffins.

She was captured, tortured, and sentenced to death — yet she refused to give up the names of those she had helped. Such was her bravery that her captors were left speechless. After the war, she spent the rest of her life quietly dedicating herself to the memory of the children she had saved. She never called herself a hero — yet to all who knew her story, she remains one of history's greatest.
1.What is the main message of this passage?
(A) War always produces heroes who seek fame. (B) True courage means acting for others despite personal danger. (C) Children are the most vulnerable victims of all conflicts. (D) Social workers should be better recognized by governments.
▶ 查看答案
✔ 正解：(B)
全文強調她不顧個人危險（"Despite the enormous danger"）仍然為他人犧牲。末段「She never called herself a hero」更凸顯無私精神，呼應 (B) 的 "acting for others despite personal danger"。
2.According to the passage, what happened to the rescuer when she was captured?
(A) She gave up the names of the people she had helped. (B) She was freed immediately due to her good reputation. (C) She was tortured and sentenced to death, but did not betray anyone. (D) She escaped from prison before the sentence was carried out.
▶ 查看答案
✔ 正解：(C)
第二段說 "She was captured, tortured, and sentenced to death — yet she refused to give up the names..."，這直接對應 (C)。注意 (A) 是反義——她「拒絕」透露名字。
3.The sentence "Such was her bravery that her captors were left speechless" uses "such" to express:
(A) A question about the quality of her bravery (B) A contrast between her and her captors (C) A result so strong that it caused an unexpected reaction (D) A condition that needed to be met for her release
▶ 查看答案
✔ 正解：(C)
這是 "such + N + that..." 的倒裝結構（Such was her bravery that...）= Her bravery was so great that...，表達結果。此句型就是今日文法重點 1，學會辨認這種倒裝！
✅ Part 4｜單字選擇複習
1.The young doctor ________ her career to providing medical care to people in remote villages.
(A) inspired(B) struggled(C) dedicated(D) sacrificed
2.________ facing many obstacles, she continued to fight for the rights of the poor and the weak.
(A) Although(B) Despite(C) However(D) Even so
3.His speech was ________ moving that many in the audience were moved to tears.
(A) such(B) very(C) so(D) too
4.It takes real ________ to speak out for justice when doing so puts you in danger.
(A) perseverance(B) sacrifice(C) bravery(D) admiration
5.After years of ________ against poverty, the family finally found a way to rebuild their lives.
(A) overcoming(B) struggling(C) inspiring(D) rescuing
📋 點此查看答案 & 解析｜手機版請調正⌄
1.(C)dedicated — dedicate A to B「把A奉獻給B」，這裡是 dedicated her career to...「將職涯奉獻於……」。(D) sacrificed 也有「犧牲」意味，但 sacrifice 後不接 to，語法不符。
2.(B)Despite — 後面接名詞片語 "facing many obstacles"（V-ing 片語），選 despite。(A) Although 後面必須接完整子句（S+V）。
3.(C)so — 後面 "moving" 是形容詞，選 so（so + adj + that）。(A) such 後面要接名詞，如 "such a moving speech that..."。
4.(C)bravery — "real bravery" 指面對危險時的勇氣，語境最符合。(A) perseverance 是「毅力」（長期堅持），(B) sacrifice 是「犧牲」，語意不同。
5.(B)struggling — "years of struggling against poverty" 多年與貧困搏鬥。struggle against 是固定片語，表示「對抗……」。
