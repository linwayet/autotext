📚 Part 1｜單字學習
scarce
[skɛrs]
adj.
稀少的；缺乏的
🔴
衍生詞scarcely (adv.) 幾乎不、僅僅｜scarcity (n.) 稀少；缺乏
容易混淆rare 強調「罕見珍貴」；scarce 強調「數量供應不足」
片語make oneself scarce 悄悄離開｜scarce resources 匱乏的資源
  • Clean water is becoming scarce in many parts of the world.
    乾淨的水在全球許多地方正逐漸稀缺。
  • Jobs were scarce during the economic downturn, and many people struggled to find work.
    經濟衰退期間工作機會稀少，許多人難以找到工作。
  • Due to the drought, food supplies grew scarce and prices rose sharply.
    由於乾旱，食物供應越來越匱乏，價格也急劇上升。
contaminate
[kənˈtæmɪˌnet]
v.
污染；使受到污染
🔴
衍生詞contamination (n.) 污染｜contaminated (adj.) 受污染的｜contaminant (n.) 污染物
容易混淆pollute 泛指空氣/水/土地污染；contaminate 強調有害物質「滲入」純淨物質
  • Factory waste had contaminated the river, killing thousands of fish.
    工廠廢棄物污染了河流，導致數千條魚死亡。
  • The soil near the old mine was contaminated with heavy metals.
    舊礦場附近的土壤受到重金屬污染。
  • Scientists warned that the chemical spill could contaminate the groundwater supply.
    科學家警告，化學品洩漏可能污染地下水供應。
habitat
[ˈhæbɪˌtæt]
n.
棲息地；自然環境
🔴
衍生詞habitation (n.) 居住地｜inhabit (v.) 居住於｜inhabitant (n.) 居民；棲居者
字根habit- (居住、持有) → inhabit 居住｜habit 習慣｜cohabit 同居
片語natural habitat 自然棲息地｜loss of habitat 棲息地喪失｜destroy habitats 破壞棲息地
  • Deforestation has destroyed the habitat of many rare birds.
    濫伐森林摧毀了許多稀有鳥類的棲息地。
  • The panda's natural habitat is limited to a few mountain ranges in China.
    貓熊的天然棲息地僅限於中國幾處山脈。
  • Protecting the habitats of endangered species is essential for biodiversity.
    保護瀕危物種的棲息地對維護生物多樣性至關重要。
resource
[rɪˈsors]
n.
資源；資產
🟢
衍生詞resourceful (adj.) 足智多謀的｜natural resources 天然資源
片語natural resources 天然資源｜human resources 人力資源｜limited resources 有限資源
  • We must use our natural resources wisely to protect the environment.
    我們必須明智地使用天然資源以保護環境。
  • The country's greatest resource is its talented and educated workforce.
    這個國家最大的資源是其有才華且受過良好教育的勞動力。
  • Schools in rural areas often lack the resources needed to provide quality education.
    農村地區的學校通常缺乏提供高品質教育所需的資源。
climate
[ˈklaɪmɪt]
n.
氣候；（社會）氛圍
🟢
衍生詞climatic (adj.) 氣候的｜climate change 氣候變化
容易混淆climate 指長期氣候規律（數年以上）；weather 指每日天氣狀況
  • Taiwan has a tropical climate with hot, humid summers and mild winters.
    台灣的氣候屬熱帶型，夏季炎熱潮濕，冬季溫和。
  • Climate change is causing more frequent and severe storms around the world.
    氣候變化正在導致全球更頻繁、更嚴重的暴風雨。
  • The economic climate affects people's spending habits and confidence.
    經濟氛圍影響人們的消費習慣和信心。
species
[ˈspiʃɪz]
n.
物種（單複數同形）
🟢
片語endangered species 瀕危物種｜invasive species 入侵物種｜native species 原生物種
容易混淆species 指生物「種」（a species / two species 單複同形）；breed 指人工培育品種
  • Over one million species are at risk of extinction due to habitat loss.
    由於棲息地喪失，超過一百萬個物種面臨滅絕的風險。
  • This species of frog is found only in the rainforests of South America.
    這種青蛙只在南美洲的熱帶雨林中才能找到。
  • Scientists discovered a new species of insect deep in the Amazon jungle.
    科學家在亞馬遜叢林深處發現了一種新的昆蟲物種。
landscape
[ˈlændˌskep]
n. / v.
（自然）景觀；地貌（v. 美化景觀）
🟢
片語the political landscape 政治格局｜urban landscape 城市景觀｜barren landscape 荒涼地貌
  • The landscape of the Scottish Highlands is breathtakingly beautiful.
    蘇格蘭高地的景觀令人歎為觀止。
  • The industrial revolution permanently changed the landscape of Britain.
    工業革命永遠改變了英國的面貌。
  • Mining activities have completely altered the landscape around the town.
    採礦活動已徹底改變了該鎮周邊的地貌。
absorb
[əbˈsɔrb]
v.
吸收；融入；全神貫注
🟢
衍生詞absorption (n.) 吸收｜absorbent (adj.) 能吸收的
片語be absorbed in 全神貫注於｜absorb carbon dioxide 吸收二氧化碳
  • Trees absorb carbon dioxide from the atmosphere and help reduce global warming.
    樹木從大氣中吸收二氧化碳，有助於減少全球暖化。
  • She was so absorbed in her book that she didn't hear the phone ring.
    她全神貫注於書本，完全沒聽到電話響。
  • The sponge can absorb a large amount of water relative to its size.
    海綿能吸收相對於其體積而言大量的水分。
sustain
[səˈsten]
v.
維持；支撐；使持續
🟢
衍生詞sustainable (adj.) 永續的｜sustainability (n.) 永續性｜unsustainable (adj.) 不可持續的
片語sustainable development 永續發展｜sustain life 維持生命｜a sustainable lifestyle 永續生活方式
  • The rainforest sustains millions of plant and animal species.
    熱帶雨林養育著數百萬種植物和動物物種。
  • We need to adopt sustainable farming methods to protect the soil for future generations.
    我們需要採用永續農業方式，為後代子孫保護土壤。
  • The athlete could not sustain her speed for the entire marathon.
    那位運動員無法在整場馬拉松中維持她的速度。
adapt
[əˈdæpt]
v.
適應；改編
🟢
衍生詞adaptation (n.) 適應；改編作品｜adaptable (adj.) 適應性強的
容易混淆adapt to 「適應環境」（主動改變自身）；adopt 「採用、收養」（接受他方的事物）
片語adapt to changes 適應變化｜adapted from 改編自
  • Animals must adapt to their environment in order to survive.
    動物必須適應環境才能生存。
  • The film was adapted from a best-selling novel by the same author.
    這部電影改編自同一位作者的暢銷小說。
  • It took her several months to adapt to life in a new country.
    她花了幾個月才適應在新國家的生活。

✏️ Part 2｜文法加強 3 大重點
文法重點 1 ｜ 段考 Q34 — 關係子句化簡（分詞構句）
關係子句可用現在分詞（V-ing）或過去分詞（pp）化簡

當關係子句動詞為主動時，化為 V-ing；若為被動，則化為 pp。化簡後句子更簡潔，學測閱讀也常見。

The river [that flows through the city] → The river flowing through the city（主動 → V-ing）
The lake [that was polluted by factories] → The lake polluted by factories（被動 → pp）
The animals living in the wetlands depend on clean water to sustain their lives.
住在溼地的動物依賴乾淨的水源維持生命。（主動 → living）
The forest damaged by the wildfire took decades to recover.
被山火破壞的森林花了數十年才恢復。（被動 → damaged）
Scientists studied the soil samples collected from the contaminated area.
科學家研究了從污染區域收集的土壤樣本。（被動 → collected）
文法重點 2 ｜ 段考 Q26 / 學測文意選填 — 因果連接詞精準搭配
due to / owing to / because of ＋ 名詞 vs. as a result / therefore ＋ 句子
【說原因】due to / owing to / because of + 名詞（片語）
【說結果】as a result / therefore / consequently + 完整句子

⚠️ due to 和 because of 後面只能接名詞片語，不能直接接子句（不能接 due to + S + V）。

Due to climate change, many species are losing their habitats.
由於氣候變化，許多物種正在失去牠們的棲息地。
The soil was contaminated; as a result, the crops failed completely.
土壤受到污染；因此，農作物完全歉收。
Water became scarce owing to the prolonged drought; therefore, the government issued warnings.
由於長期乾旱，水資源變得稀缺；因此，政府發出了警告。
文法重點 3 ｜ 學測文意選填 — 否定倒裝句（加強語氣）
以否定副詞開頭，主詞與助動詞要倒裝

當句首是 Never / Seldom / Not only / Not until / Hardly 等否定詞時，後面主詞與助動詞必須倒裝，比普通語序更有強調效果，在學測閱讀中常出現。

Never + have/has/had + S + pp ...
Not only + 助動詞 + S + V ..., but also ...
Never have scientists seen such rapid habitat loss in recorded history.
科學家在有記錄的歷史中從未見過如此迅速的棲息地喪失。
Not only does deforestation destroy animal habitats, but it also releases huge amounts of carbon dioxide.
砍伐森林不僅摧毀動物棲息地，還會釋放大量二氧化碳。
Seldom do people realize how much clean water they waste every single day.
人們很少意識到他們每天浪費了多少乾淨的水。
📖 Part 3｜閱讀測驗
主題：Environment & Nature約 114 字難度：高中

The Silent Forest

Every year, millions of hectares of forest disappear. Trees are cut down for farmland, timber, and urban development, leaving many species without a habitat. Forests do far more than provide homes for wildlife — they absorb carbon dioxide, regulate rainfall, and sustain local water supplies. When forests are lost, water becomes scarce, soils erode, and the landscape turns barren.

Many nations have pledged to protect their remaining forests, yet illegal logging continues. Scientists warn that without serious action, some of the world's most critical ecosystems could collapse within decades. The good news is that forests can recover if given the chance. Communities that adapt their farming practices and reduce their dependence on forest resources are helping to reverse the damage — one tree at a time.
1.What is the main idea of this passage?
(A) Forests should be cut for farmland. (B) Forests provide benefits but are being rapidly destroyed. (C) Tourism is the best way to fund conservation. (D) Animals easily adapt to life outside forests.
▶ 查看答案
✔ 正解：(B)
全文強調森林的多重功能（absorb CO₂ / sustain water / habitat for species）以及正遭受破壞的現況，並提出解決方向。(A)(C)(D) 均非文章重點。
2.According to the passage, which action helps reverse forest damage?
(A) Logging more trees for profit (B) Halting all tourism in forest areas (C) Communities adapting their farming and reducing resource use (D) Planting trees only in urban areas
▶ 查看答案
✔ 正解：(C)
末段明確說："Communities that adapt their farming practices and reduce their dependence on forest resources are helping to reverse the damage." 直接對應 (C)。
3.The word "scarce" in the passage is closest in meaning to:
(A) extremely cold (B) in short supply (C) heavily polluted (D) easily found
▶ 查看答案
✔ 正解：(B)
scarce = 「供應不足、稀少」→ closest to "in short supply"。(D) 是反義；(C) 是 contaminated 的意思，注意不要混淆。
✅ Part 4｜單字選擇複習
1.The area's soil had been ________ by chemicals from the nearby factory, making it unsafe for farming.
(A) adapted(B) contaminated(C) absorbed(D) sustained
2.Fresh water is becoming increasingly ________ in desert regions where rainfall is low.
(A) scarce(B) diverse(C) efficient(D) ancient
3.The destruction of forests has reduced the ________ available for tigers and other large mammals.
(A) landscape(B) climate(C) habitat(D) resource
4.Plants ________ sunlight to produce energy through the process of photosynthesis.
(A) sustain(B) absorb(C) adapt(D) disrupt
5.Over time, animals must ________ to their changing environment if they are to survive.
(A) contaminate(B) contribute(C) adopt(D) adapt
📋 點此查看答案 & 解析｜手機版請調正⌄
1.(B)contaminated — be contaminated by... 被化學品污染，是固定用法。
2.(A)scarce — 沙漠地區降雨少，水資源「稀缺」。
3.(C)habitat — 森林破壞讓動物失去「棲息地」。landscape 是景觀，意思不符。
4.(B)absorb — 植物「吸收」陽光進行光合作用，absorb sunlight 是固定搭配。
5.(D)adapt — adapt to their environment「適應環境」。注意 (C) adopt「採用、收養」不接 to，完全不同！
