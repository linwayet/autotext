📚 Part 1｜單字學習
innovative
[ˈɪnəˌvetɪv]
adj.
創新的；革新的
🔴
衍生詞innovation (n.) 創新｜innovate (v.) 進行創新｜innovator (n.) 創新者
片語innovative technology 創新技術｜innovative approach 創新方法｜innovation hub 創新中心
容易混淆innovative 強調「新穎獨特」；creative 強調「有創意、創造力」；novel (adj.) 也表示「新奇的」但較少用於科技
  • The company developed an innovative solution that reduced energy consumption by 40%.
    這家公司開發出了一種創新的解決方案，將能源消耗減少了40%。
  • Taiwan is known for its innovative semiconductor industry and skilled engineers.
    台灣以其創新的半導體產業和熟練的工程師聞名。
  • Innovation in medicine has helped doctors treat diseases that were once considered incurable.
    醫學上的創新讓醫生得以治療曾經被認為無法治癒的疾病。
accelerate
[əkˈsɛlərˌet]
v.
加速；使加快
🔴
衍生詞acceleration (n.) 加速；加速度｜accelerator (n.) 加速器；油門
容易混淆accelerate 指速度或發展「加快」；speed up 較口語；quicken 偏正式文學用語
片語accelerate growth 加速成長｜accelerate the process 加快進程｜accelerate change 促進變革
  • Artificial intelligence is accelerating changes across almost every industry.
    人工智慧正在幾乎每個行業加速推動變革。
  • The new policy was designed to accelerate economic recovery after the pandemic.
    這項新政策旨在加速疫情後的經濟復甦。
  • Climate change may accelerate the melting of polar ice, raising sea levels worldwide.
    氣候變化可能加速極地冰川融化，使全球海平面上升。
algorithm
[ˈælgəˌrɪðəm]
n.
演算法；運算規則
🔴
衍生詞algorithmic (adj.) 演算法的
片語machine learning algorithm 機器學習演算法｜social media algorithm 社群媒體演算法
  • The algorithm used by the streaming service learns what you like and recommends shows accordingly.
    串流平台使用的演算法會學習你的喜好，並據此推薦節目。
  • Engineers spent months perfecting the algorithm that detects errors in medical images.
    工程師花了幾個月完善用於偵測醫學影像錯誤的演算法。
  • Social media algorithms can influence what people read and believe about the world.
    社群媒體的演算法可以影響人們閱讀什麼以及如何看待這個世界。
device
[dɪˈvaɪs]
n.
裝置；設備；手段
🔵
片語mobile device 行動裝置｜input/output device 輸入/輸出裝置｜literary device 文學技巧
容易混淆device 指具體的機械設備；gadget 口語，指小巧的科技小物；apparatus 指複雜的儀器設備
  • Smartphones are the most widely used devices for accessing the internet today.
    智慧型手機是如今使用最廣泛的上網裝置。
  • The new device can measure heart rate and blood pressure in just seconds.
    這款新裝置可以在短短幾秒內測量心率和血壓。
  • The engineer designed a small device that could detect air pollution levels in real time.
    那位工程師設計了一款能夠即時偵測空氣污染程度的小型裝置。
network
[ˈnɛtˌwɜrk]
n. / v.
網絡；人際網絡（v. 建立人際關係）
🔵
片語social network 社交網路｜wireless network 無線網絡｜network with sb 與某人建立人脈
  • The company built a secure network to protect its employees' personal data.
    這家公司建立了一個安全的網路，以保護員工的個人資料。
  • Social networks have changed the way people communicate and share information.
    社群網路改變了人們溝通和分享資訊的方式。
  • Attending industry events is a great way to network with professionals in your field.
    參加行業活動是與同領域專業人士建立人際關係的好方法。
analyze
[ˈænəˌlaɪz]
v.
分析；解析
🔵
衍生詞analysis (n.) 分析（複數：analyses）｜analyst (n.) 分析師｜analytical (adj.) 分析性的
片語analyze data 分析數據｜carry out an analysis 進行分析｜data analyst 資料分析師
  • The AI system can analyze thousands of data points in a fraction of a second.
    這個人工智慧系統可以在極短時間內分析數千個數據點。
  • Scientists analyzed the soil samples and found traces of contamination.
    科學家分析了土壤樣本，發現了污染的痕跡。
  • A skilled analyst can turn raw data into clear and useful business insights.
    一位熟練的分析師能夠將原始數據轉化為清晰且實用的商業見解。
function
[ˈfʌŋkʃən]
n. / v.
功能；運作（n./v. 同形）
🔵
衍生詞functional (adj.) 功能性的；實用的｜malfunction (n./v.) 故障
片語function as 充當……的功能｜in full function 正常運作中｜a key function 主要功能
  • The primary function of the new app is to help users manage their daily schedules.
    這款新應用程式的主要功能是幫助用戶管理他們的日常行程。
  • The device functions like a personal assistant, answering questions and setting reminders.
    這款裝置的功能就像一位私人助理，能夠回答問題並設定提醒。
  • After the software update, the system malfunctioned and had to be restarted.
    軟體更新後，系統發生故障，必須重新啟動。
digital
[ˈdɪdʒɪtəl]
adj.
數位的；電子的
🔵
片語digital technology 數位技術｜digital divide 數位落差｜go digital 數位化
容易混淆digital 是數位的（相對於 analog 類比的）；electronic 泛指電子的（不一定是數位）
  • The digital revolution has transformed the way businesses operate around the world.
    數位革命改變了全世界企業的運作方式。
  • Many schools have adopted digital textbooks to reduce costs and improve access.
    許多學校採用了數位教科書，以降低成本並提高使用的便利性。
  • The digital divide means that not everyone has equal access to technology and the internet.
    數位落差意味著並非每個人都能平等地使用科技和網際網路。
access
[ˈæksɛs]
n. / v.
使用（權）；取得；進入（n./v. 同形）
🔵
衍生詞accessible (adj.) 易使用的；可進入的｜inaccessible (adj.) 難以使用的
片語have access to 能使用/進入……｜gain access to 獲得使用權｜access to information 取得資訊
  • Students in remote areas often struggle to access high-quality educational resources.
    偏遠地區的學生往往難以獲取高品質的教育資源。
  • The new library gives everyone free access to thousands of books and digital databases.
    這座新圖書館讓每個人都能免費使用數千本書籍和數位資料庫。
  • The website is designed to be accessible to people with visual impairments.
    這個網站的設計考慮到了視障人士的使用需求。
automate
[ˈɔtəˌmet]
v.
使自動化
🔵
衍生詞automation (n.) 自動化｜automated (adj.) 自動化的｜automatic (adj.) 自動的（偏物理機械）
容易混淆automate 動詞，強調「使某程序自動化」；automatic 是形容詞，指本身具備自動功能；automated 是過去分詞形容詞，強調已完成自動化
片語automate the process 使流程自動化｜factory automation 工廠自動化
  • Many factories have automated their assembly lines to improve efficiency and reduce costs.
    許多工廠已將其生產線自動化，以提高效率並降低成本。
  • The rise of AI may automate tasks that were once thought to require human intelligence.
    人工智慧的興起可能使曾被認為需要人類智慧才能完成的任務自動化。
  • Automation has raised concerns about job losses in manufacturing industries worldwide.
    自動化引發了人們對全球製造業就業崗位流失的擔憂。

✏️ Part 2｜文法加強 3 大重點
文法重點 1 ｜ 動名詞 vs. 不定詞 — stop to V vs. stop V-ing
stop 後面接不定詞（to V）或動名詞（V-ing），意思完全不同

這是學測和段考最愛考的不定詞 vs. 動名詞語意辨析！stop 是最典型的例子，後面接不同形式，意思180度不同。

stop + V-ing：停止做某事（之前在做，現在停下來）
stop + to V：停下來去做某事（為了做另一件事而停下）
He stopped using social media to analyze his productivity.
他停止使用社群媒體，來分析自己的生產力。（stop V-ing = 停止使用）
She stopped to check her phone when she received a notification.
她收到通知時，停下來查看手機。（stop to V = 停下來去做）
📌 記憶口訣：stop doing = 停「已做的事」；stop to do = 停下來「去做新的事」。

同類型動詞：remember / forget / try / regret 後面也有 to V vs. V-ing 的語意差異！

文法重點 2 ｜ 學測閱讀 / 段考 — 比較級的強調用法
far / much / even / still / a lot + 比較級 = 表達「更……得多」

比較級前加強調詞，可以讓語氣更強烈。這在學測閱讀中常出現，也是作文加分的好方法！

far / much / even / still / a lot / a great deal + 比較級
❌ very / so + 比較級（錯誤！很多同學都這樣寫）
AI can analyze data far more quickly than any human analyst.
人工智慧分析數據的速度比任何人類分析師快得多。
The new algorithm is much more innovative than the previous version.
新的演算法比之前的版本創新得多。
With digital tools, students can access information even faster than before.
有了數位工具，學生取得資訊的速度比以前更快了。

⚠️ 常見錯誤："very faster" / "so more efficient" — 比較級前一定用 much/far/even，不用 very/so！

文法重點 3 ｜ 學測文意選填 — not only A but (also) B 對等連接詞
not only...but also... 連接兩個對等的元素，語意加強

not only A but also B 是學測文意選填和綜合測驗常出現的結構。記住：A 和 B 必須是相同詞性的元素（都是名詞、都是動詞、都是子句等）。

not only + A + but (also) + B（A 和 B 詞性/結構需平行）
both A and B ／ either A or B ／ neither A nor B
AI has changed not only the way we work but also the way we think.
人工智慧改變的不只是我們工作的方式，也改變了我們思考的方式。（子句 + 子句 平行）
The new device is both innovative and affordable for everyday users.
這款新裝置既創新又對日常用戶來說價格實惠。（adj + adj 平行）
Not only does automation increase efficiency, but it also reduces human error significantly.
自動化不僅提高了效率，還大幅減少了人為錯誤。（倒裝：not only + 助動詞 + S）

📌 進階：not only 放句首時，後面主詞和助動詞需倒裝（連接 Day 4 文法重點 3！）

📖 Part 3｜閱讀測驗
主題：Technology & AI（連結學測科技閱讀）約 113 字難度：高中

Teaching Machines

Computers have been getting smarter for decades. Today, thanks to a technology called machine learning, computers are no longer simply following instructions — they are learning from experience. At the heart of this process is the algorithm: a set of rules that tells a computer how to analyze data and make decisions.

Machine learning has accelerated innovation in fields ranging from medicine to finance. Doctors can now use digital tools to detect diseases far earlier than before. Businesses automate repetitive tasks, freeing workers to focus on creative thinking. However, not only does this technology bring benefits, but it also raises difficult questions: What happens when machines make mistakes? Who is responsible? As AI continues to develop, these questions become far more urgent than ever.
1.What is the main idea of this passage?
(A) Machines will soon replace all human workers. (B) Machine learning brings benefits but also raises important concerns. (C) Algorithms are too complex for ordinary people to understand. (D) Digital tools are only useful in the field of medicine.
▶ 查看答案
✔ 正解：(B)
全文先說機器學習如何加速創新（benefits），末段再提出「not only...but also raises difficult questions」（concerns）。(A)(C)(D) 都只是細節或過度誇大。
2.According to the passage, how does automation benefit workers?
(A) It teaches workers how to design new algorithms. (B) It replaces workers so companies can save money. (C) It handles repetitive tasks, letting workers focus on creative thinking. (D) It gives workers access to better medical tools.
▶ 查看答案
✔ 正解：(C)
第二段說："Businesses automate repetitive tasks, freeing workers to focus on creative thinking."，直接對應 (C)。注意 (B) 是文章擔憂的面向，並非「benefit workers」。
3.The word "accelerated" in the passage is closest in meaning to:
(A) slowed down (B) replaced completely (C) sped up (D) carefully examined
▶ 查看答案
✔ 正解：(C)
accelerate = 「加速」→ closest to "sped up"。(A) 是反義。注意文中說 AI "accelerated innovation"（加速創新），而非減緩或取代。
✅ Part 4｜單字選擇複習
1.The doctor used an ________ approach to treat the patient, combining AI tools with traditional medicine.
(A) digital(B) automated(C) innovative(D) accessible
2.The new software can ________ large amounts of medical data to identify potential health risks.
(A) access(B) accelerate(C) automate(D) analyze
3.She ________ checking her emails every five minutes and found she was much more productive.
(A) stopped to check(B) stopped checking(C) stop to check(D) stop checking
4.The internet has made information ________ more accessible to people in remote areas than it was decades ago.
(A) very(B) so(C) far(D) too
5.The new ________ on the smartphone allows users to make payments without carrying a physical wallet.
(A) network(B) function(C) algorithm(D) innovation
📋 點此查看答案 & 解析｜手機版請調正⌄
1.(C)innovative — "innovative approach" 創新方法。(A) digital 是「數位的」；(B) automated 是「自動化的」，語意不符。
2.(D)analyze — 「分析大量醫療數據」。(A) access = 取得使用權；(B) accelerate = 加速；(C) automate = 使自動化，語意皆不符。
3.(B)stopped checking — 她「停止了查看郵件的習慣」，後來發現更有效率。stop V-ing = 停止做某事。(A) stopped to check = 停下來去查看（之後繼續查看），語意相反！
4.(C)far — far more accessible = 更容易取得得多。比較級前只能用 far/much/even，不能用 very 或 so！
5.(B)function — 「功能」讓使用者無需實體錢包即可付款。(A) network 是網路；(C) algorithm 是演算法；(D) innovation 是創新（太抽象）。
